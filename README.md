# 💳 Credit Recovery

> **Understand your credit health. Get a personalized recovery plan.**

Credit Recovery is a fintech web application designed to help users understand the factors affecting their credit health and take actionable steps toward improving it.

Instead of simply showing a credit score, the platform analyzes important credit-related factors and generates a personalized recovery plan with practical goals.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Many people with a low credit score know **what their score is**, but don't know:

- Why their credit health is poor
- Which financial habits are affecting them
- What they should improve first
- What actions they should take over the next few months

Existing solutions often focus on **checking the score**, rather than helping users **recover and improve** it.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**Credit Recovery** converts credit-related information into an easy-to-understand health assessment and a personalized action plan.

The application:

1. Collects basic financial and credit information
2. Calculates credit utilization
3. Generates a **Credit Health Score (0–100)**
4. Identifies areas that need improvement
5. Creates personalized recovery recommendations
6. Provides a roadmap for improving financial habits
7. Stores user analysis and recovery data using Firebase

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Credit Health Analysis

Users can enter:

- Credit score
- Monthly income
- Credit limit
- Outstanding balance
- Late payments
- Active loans
- Recent credit inquiries

The application analyzes these factors and generates a Credit Health Score.
