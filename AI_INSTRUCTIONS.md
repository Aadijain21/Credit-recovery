# AI Development Instructions

## Before Editing

Always inspect:

- README.md
- PROJECT_CONTEXT.md
- FEATURES.md
- ARCHITECTURE.md
- TODO.md

before making major changes.

## Rules

1. Do not rewrite working code unnecessarily.
2. Preserve existing features.
3. Prefer simple solutions.
4. Avoid adding unnecessary frameworks.
5. Keep the project suitable for a hackathon.
6. Explain major changes before implementing them.
7. Test functionality after changes.
8. Do not expose secrets or credentials.
9. Do not claim the Credit Health Score is the official CIBIL score.
10. Keep Firebase operations separate from UI logic where practical.

## Tech Restrictions

Current stack:

HTML
CSS
JavaScript
Firebase Firestore

Do not introduce React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, or other frameworks
unless explicitly requested.

## Product Principle

The goal is:

"Don't just tell users their credit health.
Tell them what to do next."
